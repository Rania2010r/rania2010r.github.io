## 🔍 How to Use pdf-sign

Using pdf-sign is straightforward. Here’s how to sign a PDF:

### Step 1: Open Your PDF

1. Launch pdf-sign from your applications.
2. Click on “Open” to select the PDF document you want to sign.

### Step 2: Add Your Signature

1. After the PDF is open, look for the "Sign" option in the menu.
2. Select your signing method. You can choose:
   - GPG signature
   - Sigstore signature (supports Google, GitHub, Microsoft accounts)
3. Follow the prompts to create or add your signature.

### Step 3: Save the Signed PDF

Once your signature is added, click “Save” to keep your signed document. It will now be tamper-proof and ready for sharing.
